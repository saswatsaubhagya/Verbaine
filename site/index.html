Verbaine
How it works Privacy GitHub

Better writing in every Mac app.

Select text in Slack, Mail, Notes or your browser, press ⌃⌥P, and Verbaine fixes, rewrites or summarizes it right there. The model runs on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Download for Mac

Free. Apple Silicon, macOS 26.4 or later, Apple Intelligence on.

Three keystrokes, no copy-paste

  1. Select

    Highlight text in any app, including Slack, Chrome and other apps the built-in Writing Tools skip.

  2. Press ⌃⌥P

    A small panel opens next to your selection. Pick an action.

  3. Replace or copy

    Changed words are highlighted. Replace puts the result back in place, and ⌘Z still undoes it.

What it can do

Fix grammar
Spelling, grammar and punctuation only. Your words and tone stay.
Improve
Clearer and more natural, about the same length.
Summarize
Three bullets or two sentences. Long threads are summarized in parts.
Shorten
Cuts about 40% and keeps every fact.
Change tone
Professional, friendly, direct or apologetic.
Expand
Turns bullet points or a terse note into full sentences.

Private by default

Verbaine uses Apple's on-device model, which already ships with macOS. No account, no subscription, no server.

Prefer a bigger model? You can point Verbaine at your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Your key stays in the Keychain, and the menu bar shows when text is leaving your Mac.

Installing

Verbaine isn't notarized yet, so macOS asks you to approve it once.

  1. Download the latest release and move Verbaine.app to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the app — that's expected.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Open Verbaine again, then grant Accessibility access when asked so it can read your selection.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26.4 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings.
